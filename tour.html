Step 1 · Blitz
三みっつの ラウンド、三みっつの 舞台ぶたい
Three timed rounds, three stages

Words in a circus tent, sentences in a spooky forest, questions in a firefly glade. Each round has a target time and remembers your best. Clear all three and the Maze welcomes you in.

Step 2 · The Maze
迷路めいろの 中なかに、9つのゲーム
Nine games inside the maze

The Maze opens onto three trees, one for each track: Pre-Boo, Boo-riculum, and Boo-Continuum. Each tree holds the same nine games at its own level: three for words, three for sentences, three for listening and speaking.

NEW★★★
にじの 星ほしは、準備じゅんびしてきた しるし

Students who come prepared and clear the week's maze games at a ready pace earn rainbow stars instead of gold. Gold means the game is done. Rainbow means you studied before you played.

Step 4 · Extra practice
おまけの 2つと、ウィークボス
Two practice games and a boss fight

Booha Bites: Booha ate one word from each sentence, find it. Echo: hear a word, type it. Week Boss: fifteen rounds of this week's English in new formats, with critical hits for streaks. Win the week's crown.

These are bonus practice. They never change the nine-game unlock.

Homework and Daily Check
宿題しゅくだいと、毎日まいにちの ランタン
Homework and the Lantern Path

Homework is this week's words and sentences to copy into a notebook, with audio, a print view, a test mode that hides the English, and a projector mode for class. A short Daily Check lights one lantern a day.

迷路めいろの 先さきの 世界せかいThe Worlds

それぞれの 世界せかいが、ちがう 遊あそび方かたで 英語えいごを 使つかいます。

Each world uses English in a different way: collecting, reading, investigating, typing, and spotting changes.

🔒9 / 9 Finish all nine maze games to open Utsuroba, Muenba, Grimmerglen, and Akiya for the week.

カラスキKARASUKI

忘わすれられたものが 行いく 世界せかい。

Where forgotten things go. Finish a maze game and wanderers appear: friendly, mischievous, or dangerous. Thirty-six to find, and travelers like Nuppi stop by too.

うつろばUTSUROBA

消きえない 記憶きおくの 部屋へや。

A room for what stayed. Six drifters each hold one memory told in English. Read it at Starter, Case, or Deep depth and give it back.

むえんばMUENBA

きりの へやが 15こ。ゴーストが 5たい。

Fifteen foggy rooms, five ghosts hiding. Earn Nuppi's trust, read the case notes, and catch them before Sunday.

グリマーグレンGRIMMERGLEN

マリエッタは きもちは おぼえている。

Marietta remembers the feeling, but not always the thing. Short typing challenges pull memories out of everyday objects, with 24 clues to find each week.

New · Akiya
あきや、めを そらさないで。
Akiya: don't blink

Study a room in an empty Japanese house while the light is on. Then it goes dark. Move Booha to whatever changed, mark it, and report the room. The house opens one room at a time, starting at the genkan, and each room can be patient, quicker, or one that lies to you.

Bronze for showing up, silver for finishing, gold for doing it well. Each one adds bonus stars: +3, +5, or +10.

Stars and links
となりどうしが ★★ で、みちが ひかる
Neighbors at two stars light the path

On the Adventure Log the nine games form a trail. Play them in any order. When two neighbors both reach two stars, the link between them lights up for a bonus star. Every star counts toward a lifetime level that only goes up, and prepared students turn their stars rainbow.

Adventure tokens
ずっと 残のこる トークン
Tokens that stay forever

Permanent tokens for the whole journey, the student's own track, Muenba, and Grimmerglen. Crown Collector, Boss Buster, Wanderer Host and others have tiers to climb, and some are secret until earned.

About

Bryan Harper is from Columbus, Ohio. He first came to Japan as a firefighter in the United States Air Force, stationed at Yokota Air Base. He met his wife there, then later returned to Japan and put down roots.

When his Air Force service ended, Bryan began teaching English. He worked as an ALT at elementary, junior high, and high schools, taught at international schools, and gave private lessons. He also led workshops and events. In each setting, he kept asking why so many students could study English for years and still feel unable to have a conversation. That question stayed with him and eventually led him to open his own school.

Bryan opened his English school in Tomisato, Chiba with a simple idea: students need to use English, not just take tests. That idea shaped the Output Lesson, where students prepare at home and then speak in a small group. It also led to Booha Adventure, a weekly learning platform he built from scratch. The same materials are now used in English Juku, where students practise the English they have studied that week on their own devices and show what they can do.

Bryan teaches English. He also builds furniture, runs workshops, illustrates characters, and designs card games. He enjoys making things he wishes existed and sharing them with people who enjoy making things too.

Karasuki is where forgotten things go. Bryan has been writing the novel for years, long before Booha Adventure existed. He later brought Karasuki into the app, where he could add characters, side stories, and parts of the world that aren't in the novel. Make of that what you will.
